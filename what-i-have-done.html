What I have done

Consultant or product manager, the title has never changed the work: I make sense of the abstract, work through the chaos to something real, and translate it so every function can see it and get behind it. Here is the journey - three companies, three countries, eighteen years.

Overview What I have done Skills behind the journey

7 years
South Africa, India & UK
