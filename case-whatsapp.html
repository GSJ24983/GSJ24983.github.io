Groups within groups - a moderation model for WhatsApp

An end-to-end feature recommendation: a group-to-subgroup tree, plus a new Moderator role, so large communities could finally be structured and moderated.

Why this case matters

Proposed in 2020. Shipped by WhatsApp in 2022.

I specified a group-to-subgroup structure with admin and moderator roles and announcements in early 2020. WhatsApp announced Communities - its own 'groups within groups', with sub-groups, admin controls and announcement groups - in April 2022, and rolled it out globally that November. Same shape, roughly two and a half years earlier. This isn't a write-up of an existing feature; it's the record of calling one before the platform did.

Why

The idea, and why it mattered

Big groups don't scale. My proposal: nest groups into sub-groups, and split authority with a new Moderator role alongside Admin.

Market

Messaging is where people go

Users increasingly message businesses instead of calling - communities need structure.

Size

~$9.6B space

Timing

Maturity needs growth

WhatsApp sat at product maturity - a moment to expand into new use-cases.

Who · competitor analysis

Benchmarked against best-in-class

Group management

Structure, roles, moderation - Discord clearly ahead.

Communication

Announcements, threading, pinning - gaps on WhatsApp's side.

Tooling

Who · persona analysis

Three groups the feature is for

Nesting and moderation aren't abstract - each persona feels the pain of unstructured big groups differently.

Business

The small business owner

Runs customer groups that grow unmanageable; needs sub-groups and roles to keep order and respond.

Product

The community / product manager

Runs a large user community; needs announcements, moderation and structure without a full platform.

Education

The educator / institute

One cohort, many batches; needs a parent group with sub-groups per batch and controlled posting.

How · feature breakdown

Specified like a PRD, not a slide of ideas

Personas and goals, UX mockups, permissions, user stories - the full spec. At its core, a clean split of authority.
