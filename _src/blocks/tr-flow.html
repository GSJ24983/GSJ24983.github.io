How an engagement worksFind the gaps, train them, check they closed

For corporate teams, MBA programmes and individuals. The same method measures before training and after it, so the movement is visible.

Step 0 · Strings

Find the gaps

Each person rates themselves on the anchors, then works through short scenarios and writes two things: their read of the situation and what they would say. The trainer scores the writing against a rubric you can see. The gap between the two - how you see yourself and what you do under pressure - is where we start.

Step 1 · Train

Work the anchors that matter

Scenario-based sessions on the priority anchors, as live workshops or a cohort. Priorities come from the gaps, not from a guess.

Step 2 · Strings again

See what moved

About four weeks after training, a different scenario on the same anchors, rated the same way. You see whether the gap closed.

Strings is the Swanubhava™ diagnostic. Its ratings guide development conversations; they are not a hiring test or a validated psychometric score. The way forward is shaped by the gaps each group shows. For formats and pricing, get in touch.
