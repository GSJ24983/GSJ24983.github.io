Training engagements

Remote or on-site
Remote by default. I am happy to be on-site in Pune, and to travel for workshops and key sessions.
How an engagement starts
  1. Step 0 - Strings (assessment before training): each person rates themselves on the anchors, then works through scenarios and writes their read of the situation and what they would say, which the trainer scores against the rubric. The gap between the self-rating and the scenario rating shows where to focus. A target level is optional and, if wanted, is set together with me.
  2. Priority anchors: agree the Swanubhava™ anchors to focus on first, from the gaps.
  3. Engagement finalisation: short-term engagement or long-term engagement (cohort).
Formats
  1. Short-term engagement: workshops on up to 3 priority anchors, one session each - live scenarios, then a group walkthrough against the behaviour rubric.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): a cohort works through its priority anchors over several weeks, with Strings at the start and again at the end to show movement. MBA students work in groups of 4-5; corporate participants individually.

Some people need 1:1 coaching rather than training. Strings shows which, and I can point you to the right coach.

Typical duration
  1. Short-term engagement: half a day to a full day, for 8-20 people.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): 6-8 weeks for 10-15 people.

These are rough batch sizes and durations. They can be changed to fit your requirement.

Built around your context
The three pillars and eight anchors stay the same. The behavioural traits and scenarios can be tailored, and I can help you build scenarios from your own situations under each pillar.
After training - Strings again
About four weeks after the last session, a different scenario on the same anchors, rated the same way, to show whether the gap has closed.
