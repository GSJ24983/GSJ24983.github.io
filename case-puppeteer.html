Case 03 · 0→1 concept & blue-ocean strategy

The Puppeteer - content where the viewer pulls the strings

A product concept for a crowded OTT market: Contentdt - content embedded with a decision tree. At each crucial moment, the viewer chooses what the character does. One story becomes many. This is the concept worked end to end - from the blank idea to a blue-ocean strategy, STP and a product spec.

Why

What is Contentdt?

Imagine watching a movie - and controlling what the characters do. At a crucial juncture, the character has to make a critical decision, and you make it. Should Raj elope with Simran, or not? You become the puppeteer who pulls the strings, and the story branches from your choice.

The shift

From watching to deciding

Passive viewing becomes active choice - the viewer is inside the story, not in front of it.

The form

One film, many films

A single piece of content holds many short paths - re-watchable, because each run is different.

The hook

Content that challenges you

Every decision node is a small puzzle - entertainment that engages the intellect, not just the eyes.

Approach

The OTT market is a red ocean

Everyone competes on the same factors - content volume, proprietary shows, no ads, price, live. I mapped the incumbents on a strategy canvas to find the empty water: a factor nobody offers.

Netflix, Hotstar and YouTube compete on the same seven factors (India). Interactivity - letting the viewer decide - is a factor none of them offer.

The blue-ocean move

Compete on a factor nobody else has

Rather than out-spend the incumbents on content, ads or price, Contentdt opens a new axis: interactive, branching content. It's a differentiator no current player offers - and one that turns a viewer into a participant, driving re-watches and engagement the others can't match.

How · the demo

The Honest Woodcutter, as branching content

To show the format, I storyboarded a familiar tale as Contentdt. The same story yields very different endings - the viewer's choices write the path.

One story, several endings - each a different path through the same content. That is Contentdt.

How · product spec

Specified for both sides of the platform

For the viewer

Watch, decide, save, share

  • Register, subscribe, browse and wishlist
  • Play, pause, stop - and log every decision
  • Save decision nodes to resume a path
  • Thumbs up/down and a community for hidden Easter eggs
For the creator

Build and validate a decision tree

  • Add segments and decision nodes with action buttons
  • Link each button to the next segment; preview the flow
  • Review tool flags missing links before publish
  • Timers, default actions, and group voting (majority, weighted, in-app or SMS)
Outcome · the model

How it makes money, and where it grows

Revenue

Four streams

  • Premium tiers - extra decisions, weighted group-watch

Extensions

Beyond entertainment

  • Decision-based games
  • Assessment through choices, not questions
