Safari AI Assistant

A multimodal assistant for Tata Safari owners: ask a question in plain language, or upload a photo of a dashboard warning light, and get an answer grounded only in the official service and infotainment manuals - so it explains, it doesn't hallucinate. Built solo, end to end, and used as the first real test case for my AI Trustworthiness Matrix - so I know precisely where it is strong and where it is not.
