Original frameworks

Methods that turn opinion into a decision

Framework · built on NIST AI RMF

AI Trustworthiness Matrix

Rate eight AI-trust dimensions - then pair each rating with how confident you are in it and what you actually inspected. The action comes from the cell, not the score: a low score you're sure of and a high score you're guessing about demand very different responses. It turns a vague sense that a model is 'probably fine' into a measured, prioritised fix-list. First proved on my own Safari build, so I know exactly where it's strong and where it isn't.

Framework · in use

PKVit prioritisation

Scores features on a painkiller-to-vitamin spectrum - how sharply each relieves real pain versus how much it merely adds - to force a clean ranking when everything looks urgent. It makes the trade-off legible to stakeholders who are otherwise arguing from gut, and gives a defensible reason for what ships first.

Framework · primary research

Goal-based travel-package model

A first-of-its-kind multi-criteria and affinity scoring model that assembles a travel package around a traveller's goal, not a destination. Backed by 100-user primary research, with no known market equivalent - a genuinely new way to shape the offer.

White paper

AHP-based content scheduling

Applied the Analytic Hierarchy Process - a statistical multi-criteria model - to a content-scheduling decision people were arguing from opinion. Authored as a white paper: a subjective fight turned into a weighted, defensible decision anyone can audit.

Contributor · standard-setting

IIBA Product Ownership Analysis guide

Officially credited contributor to the IIBA's global CPOA guide - a standard the profession builds on. Not a framework I use, but one I helped write.

Speaker · IPA Summit

'The Sherlock Way'

On simplifying complex business processes for intelligent process automation - my working method for cutting a tangled process down to what actually matters, turned into a talk.
