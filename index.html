About me

18+ years of shaping, shipping and aligning

I get handed problems that don't have a shape yet - five times from a blank page, in five industries - and turn them into products a team can run. The role has changed (BA, consultant, PM, VP); the move hasn't: frame the real problem, build something real, align every function behind it.

Most recently I led a $25M/month travel and loyalty platform at JPMorgan Chase through an on-prem-to-cloud rebuild. Before that, product and transformation work across Cybage and Nihilent - in India, South Africa and the UK.

The through-line now is AI product leadership - building real capability with my own hands, grounded enough to defend the stack in front of a CIO.
