How we’d work

Consulting engagements

Remote or on-site
Remote by default. I am happy to be on-site in Pune, and to travel for workshops and key sessions.
How an engagement starts
  1. A free 30-minute problem conversation - over email, a call or face to face.
  2. I listen to what’s going on, ask the questions that sharpen the problem, and say honestly whether I’m the right fit.
  3. Engagement finalisation: if there’s a fit, a short written scope follows, with the outcome, format, duration and what ‘done’ looks like.
Formats
The format depends on the problem to be solved during the assignment.
Typical duration
Discovery sprints usually run 2-4 weeks. Fractional roles run 3-6 months, with an option to extend. Advisory retainers are month-to-month.
Online or in person
Both. Most of the work runs well online. Workshops and key sessions can be in person in Pune, or in other cities by arrangement.
How we’d work

Training & coaching engagements

Remote or on-site
Remote by default. I am happy to be on-site in Pune, and to travel for workshops and key sessions.
How an engagement starts
  1. Assessment to identify gaps in the product management competencies of individuals and/or teams.
  2. Priority anchors: agree the Swanubhava™ anchors to focus on first.
  3. Engagement finalisation: short-term engagement, long-term engagement (cohort), or coaching engagement.
Formats
  1. Short-term engagement: 4 one-hour workshops for product teams (roughly 4-6 scenarios covering 4 competencies) - live scenarios, then a group walkthrough of each decision against the behaviour rubric.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): a group works through all eight behaviours over several weeks, with assessment at the start and end to show movement.
  3. Coaching engagement: 1:1 coaching for individual PMs and product leaders, anchored on the behaviours they most want to strengthen.
Typical duration
  1. Short-term engagement: half a day to a full day, for 8-20 people.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): 6-8 weeks for 10-15 people, roughly one behaviour per week.
  3. Coaching engagement: a series of 6-8 sessions over 2-3 months.
Online or in person
Both. The scenarios run in the browser and work on a phone, so online delivery works well. Workshops can be in person in Pune, or in other cities by arrangement.
