My training method

Swanubhava™ trains how product people behave when it gets hard.

Most product management training teaches what a product manager delivers: PRDs, roadmaps, backlogs. Swanubhava™ trains the behaviour behind the work - and measures it by the decisions people make.

See a scenario ↓
How an engagement runs

Start by finding the gaps. Finish by checking they closed.

For corporate teams, MBA programmes and individuals. The same method measures before training and after it, so the movement is visible.

Step 0 · Strings

Find the gaps

Each person rates themselves on the anchors, then works through a scenario and writes what they would actually do. The trainer rates that answer against the rubric. The gap between the two - how you see yourself and what you do under pressure - is where we start.

Step 1 · Train

Work the anchors that matter

Scenario-based sessions on the priority anchors, as live workshops, a cohort or 1:1 coaching. Priorities come from the gaps, not from a guess.

Step 2 · Strings again

See what moved

A different scenario on the same anchors, rated the same way. You see whether the gap closed.

Strings is the Swanubhava™ diagnostic. Its ratings guide development conversations; they are not a hiring test or a validated psychometric score. For formats, group sizes and pricing, get in touch.

Content · what we train

Three postures, eight anchors

Three postures of a product manager: on their own, then the boundaries they hold, then the relations they build. Each pillar has behavioural anchors; each anchor breaks into ground-level behaviours, trained as scenarios. Tap an anchor to hear what it sounds like.

Behaviours overlap by design - real situations rarely test just one. Inspired by the ICF Core Competencies and rebuilt for product work.

Why scenarios, not slides

Built on how experts actually decide

<12%

of 156 decisions by experienced fire commanders involved comparing options. They recognised the situation and acted.

Klein, Calderwood & Clinton-Cirocco, 1986

studies of simulation-based learning showed a large positive effect on complex skills.

Write it

People remember what they produce better than what they read - so learners write their own response before seeing the model answer.

Bertsch et al., 2007

How we’d work

Training & coaching engagements

Remote or on-site
Remote by default. I am happy to be on-site in Pune, and to travel for workshops and key sessions.
How an engagement starts
  1. Step 0 - Strings (assessment before training): each person rates themselves on the anchors, then works through a scenario and writes their answer, which the trainer rates against the rubric. The gap between the self-rating and the scenario rating shows where to focus. A target level is optional and, if wanted, is set together with me.
  2. Priority anchors: agree the Swanubhava™ anchors to focus on first, from the gaps.
  3. Engagement finalisation: short-term engagement, long-term engagement (cohort), or coaching engagement.
Formats
  1. Short-term engagement: 4 one-hour workshops for product teams (roughly 4-6 scenarios covering 4 anchors) - live scenarios, then a group walkthrough of each decision against the behaviour rubric.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): a group works through all eight behaviours over several weeks, with Strings at the start and a second scenario at the end to show movement.
  3. Coaching engagement: 1:1 coaching for individual PMs and product leaders, anchored on the behaviours they most want to strengthen.
Typical duration
  1. Short-term engagement: half a day to a full day, for 8-20 people.
  2. Long-term engagement (cohort): 6-8 weeks for 10-15 people, roughly one behaviour per week.
  3. Coaching engagement: a series of 6-8 sessions over 2-3 months.
After training - Strings again
A different scenario on the same anchors, rated the same way, to show whether the gap has closed.
Pricing
It depends on the format and the size of the group. Please get in touch for details.
Online or in person
Both. The scenarios run in the browser and work on a phone, so online delivery works well. Workshops can be in person in Pune, or in other cities by arrangement.
