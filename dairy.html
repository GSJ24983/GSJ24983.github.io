Case study · Product strategy & platform thinking

A struggling dairy farm, turned into a multi-tenant platform

A family-run organic dairy couldn't predict demand and took every order by hand. Asked how a product strategist would fix it, I went from a blank prompt to a product - and then to a platform any dairy could run.

Product strategist

The problem

One prompt, three real failures - all rooted in guesswork and paper.

Order management

No demand visibility

Surplus one day, unfulfilled orders the next. Waste on both sides.

Manual process

Orders on paper

The front desk logged every call in a notebook - lost details, wrong addresses.

Experience

Late orders dropped

Anything after the daily cut-off simply didn't get delivered.

The approach - frame before you fix

Before proposing anything, I mapped the whole business on a 4C canvas - so the solution answered the real system, not just the loudest symptom.

What I designed

A technology product for the dairy - the order journey, wrapped in the operational layers it actually needs.

Core journey

Search → order → pay → supply

One clean flow across web, app and the front desk.

Operating layers

Reports, engagement, exceptions

Analytics, customer engagement, and explicit exception handling for the edge cases.

People

8 personas across the chain

From collector and dispatcher to storage, fulfilment and admin.

The leap - from a product to a platform

The real insight: solving one dairy's problem builds the thing every dairy needs. I redrew the single-client product as a multi-tenant platform.

Product - one client

Organic Dairy Farm

  • Search, order, pay, supply
  • Reports & analytics
  • Exception handling
  • Serves a single farm
Platform - any client

Any dairy farm

  • Client hierarchy management
  • Client revenue management
  • Per-tenant catalogue & config
  • Per-tenant UI modules
