Who I am

Five cold starts, in five industries. Each one without a roadmap, a dataset or a team.

The title has changed - consultant, analyst, product owner, VP. The move hasn’t: frame the real problem, build something real, align every function behind it.

Six results, each tagged with the beat of Shape-Ship-Synchronize it came from.

ShipMoved every client to the cloud, broke nothing.
100% adoption

ShipInherited a stalled workstream and drove it to outcome.

AI, on the job and off it

Where AI has earned a place in the actual work - and what I build on my own.

3 causal loops

Found what the detractors were really saying.

Analysed detractor voice-of-customer data, supplier content and regulatory text to surface three loops driving compliance risk and NPS damage. Turned them into two validated solutions: a readability redesign of lodging policy and cancellation content, and automated scanning of supplier content against regulatory sources to flag risky terms and trigger action. Both went to the owning teams, and the scanning concept is on the AI team’s backlog.
